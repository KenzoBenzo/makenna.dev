# Surface brief: home (makenna.dev)

Scope: home page, with the same world carried into /projects (stories), /blog and their detail pages. Mode: Experience. Audience: investors and partners, hiring managers and clients, peers. Job: understand that Makenna builds whole businesses (Cirrus 9 Candy, ShelfFlow, design engineering) and reach out or read a story. Proof: real product screenshots, product photos, press (sofi finalist, GMA), shipped dates. Constraint: no invented traction; ShelfFlow website stats and deck market numbers are not shown as facts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Makenna as a product line. The page is a CPG wholesale line sheet where each venture is a SKU with its spec, proof and story; it refuses the dark mono dev-portfolio with a hero and a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: white offset sheet, black ink, heavy black masthead rule and hairline row rules, spot red (#d7262e) for the order action and new items, process cyan for SKU codes and links. Archivo condensed heavy for display, Archivo for body, JetBrains Mono only for SKU codes, dates and data. Tabular figures everywhere numbers sit in columns. Product thumbnails sit in rows, never in floating cards.

STORY: the visitor reads her name and claim, scans the line (ShelfFlow, Cirrus 9 Candy, design engineering) with proof per row, opens a row's spec or its story, browses the full catalog of past roles filtered by skill, meets the maker, and places an order (gets in touch).

FIRST VIEWPORT: masthead band (MCS lockup, "Line sheet, Fall/Winter 2026", Westchester NY, nav) over a giant condensed name across the full width with the one-line claim; below it the line table header and its first two rows with thumbnails, the red "Place an order" action at the masthead's right.

FORM: Wholesale Line Sheet, my top-ranked grounded candidate (pick card) chosen over the roll's assignment. seed:97d912578dab9cee

Signature interaction: a line row opens in place into its full spec (specs list, resources, story link) with a height and clip reveal; one motion moment, ease-out, reduced-motion respected.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
